THE RULES

Words must be at least 4 letters long and contain the centre letter. Letters may be reused. Four-letter words earn 1 point; longer words earn 1 point per letter. Use all 7 letters for a 7-point pangram bonus.

An independent word game inspired by classic letter-hive puzzles. No account required. Dictionary: ESDB, Australian English. Dictionary copyright and licence. Hint definitions adapted from Open English WordNet 2025 (CC BY 4.0).

How to play

Build words from the seven letters. Each word must use the golden centre letter, be at least four letters long, and use only the letters shown. Repeated letters are allowed.

Score 1 point for four-letter words, 1 point per letter for longer words, and a bonus 7 points for words using all seven letters.

Find as many words as you can and aim for the highest rank. Try the daily puzzle, or choose New puzzle whenever you like. Previous takes you back, and Today’s puzzle returns to the daily challenge. Each puzzle’s progress is saved separately on this device.

Answers come from ESDB level 60 using Australian spelling. Words may differ from those accepted by other word games.
