THE RULES

Words must be at least 4 letters long and contain the centre letter. Letters may be reused. Four-letter words earn 1 point; longer words earn 1 point per letter. Use all 7 letters for a 7-point pangram bonus.

An independent word game inspired by classic letter-hive puzzles. No account required.

How to play

Build words from the seven letters. Each word must use the golden centre letter, be at least four letters long, and use only the letters shown. Repeated letters are allowed.

Score 1 point for four-letter words, 1 point per letter for longer words, and a bonus 7 points for words using all seven letters.

Find as many words as you can and aim for the highest rank. There is a new letter set each day, and your progress is saved locally.

The offline dictionary is expanded and curated, though acceptance can still differ from other word games.
